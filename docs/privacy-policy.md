# Proxyn Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Proxyn is an app for monitoring and managing Proxmox VE servers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary

Proxyn does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no Proxyn account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Information stored on your device

To connect to your servers, Proxyn stores the details you enter — server names and addresses, usernames, authentication realms, API token identifiers and trusted certificate fingerprints — on your device. Passwords and API token secrets are stored in the iOS Keychain as device-only items, so they are not included in device backups.

This information is shared between the Proxyn app and its widgets on the same device through an App Group. It is never sent to the developer.

## Communication with your servers

Proxyn connects directly from your device to the Proxmox VE servers you add. Requests are not routed through any server operated by the developer. Anything those servers record is under the control of their administrator.

## Data collected by the developer

None. Proxyn contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or crash-reporting libraries. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymized crash reports, under Apple's privacy policy.

## Children

Proxyn is a tool for people who administer servers and is not directed at children.
